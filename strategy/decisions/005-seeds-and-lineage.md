# Decision 005: Seeds and lineage

**Date:** 2026-10-12
**Status:** Accepted
**Decider:** Cruz
**Related:** [Decision 001](001-pipeline-contract.md) · [Decision 003](003-remove-unmeasured-outcome-claims.md)

## Context

Release retrospectives in the Dojo Genesis practice recorded lessons Git for Strategy never carried. The Liberation Psychology seed cited Fanon without a source, and the lineage the practice documents for itself — beginner's mind, understanding before help, self-definition — appeared nowhere in the repository.

## Options considered

| Option | What you get | What you give up | Risk | Cost to undo |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Add some of the retro lessons | A smaller library | Lessons the practice already paid for | Low | Low |
| Add all four groups | Grounding, deciding, honesty, execution | A longer index | Low | Low |
| Liberation Psychology: cite it properly | A sourced foundation | — | Low | Low |
| Liberation Psychology: re-ground in the documented lineage | Continuity with the practice | — | Low | Low |
| Liberation Psychology: drop it | A shorter list | A foundation of the practice | Medium | Low |

### What was not chosen, and why

Dropping the seed. Cruz chose both to keep it, cited, and to ground the human-agency commitment in the lineage the practice documents — so that became a seed of its own.

## Decision

Add all four groups of retro seeds. Keep Liberation Psychology, with sources (Martín-Baró, Fanon). Add Partnership, Not Autopilot, grounded in Shunryu Suzuki, Thich Nhat Hanh, and Patricia Hill Collins. Remove every unsourced figure from the seeds.

## Ruling

> Seeds: "Grounding", "Deciding", "Honesty", "Execution".
>
> Lineage: "Re ground in documented lineage and keep and city [cite] properly"
>
> Collins: "Patricia Hill Collins"

— Cruz, 2026-10-12. The seed groups and Collins were selected from options the agent wrote; the lineage ruling is in his words, with "[cite]" marking a typo.

## Consequences

The seed library gains groups and an index generated from disk. Two foundation seeds carry published sources.
