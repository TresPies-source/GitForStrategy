## Question

How often do inspectors work without a usable connection, and what do they need the device to do there?

## Claims

| Claim | Source | Date | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Inspectors routinely work in basements and plant rooms with no signal. | Interviews with inspectors (illustrative) | 2026-03-03 | Medium — a small number of interviews |
| Photos are taken on most inspections, but uploading can wait. | Same interviews | 2026-03-03 | Medium |
| Nobody asked for device features beyond the camera. | Same interviews | 2026-03-03 | Low — one round of questions |

## Disagreements

One inspector preferred paper in the field and typing up later. Recorded, not resolved.

## Gaps

How often signal is lost was described, not measured. A short trial with logging on real devices would measure it.
