# Ask Where the Work Happens

*Ask where the work happens before choosing where the software runs.*

## What it is

Before a platform decision, find out where people will actually use the software: the place, the connection, the light, their hands.

## Why it matters

Platform debates run on features. The deciding fact is often the setting — no signal, gloves on, one hand free.

## The pattern

1. Ask, or better, watch, where the work happens.
2. Write down the conditions as claims, with sources.
3. Scout routes against those conditions.

## Revisit when

A platform or device decision is on the table.

## What it refuses

Choosing a platform from a feature list alone.
