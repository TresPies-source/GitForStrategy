# Measure the Corrective

*When a fix works, measure what it changed.*

## What it is

When a corrective practice is added after a failure, measure its effect against the failure it was meant to stop.

## Why it matters

Correctives that are never measured become rituals, kept or dropped by mood. A measured one earns its place, or shows that it has not.

## The pattern

1. Name the failure the corrective addresses.
2. Before the next run, decide what you will compare.
3. Compare against the earlier baseline.
4. Write the result in the retro, including "no difference."

## Revisit when

You add a new checklist, gate, or review step.

## What it refuses

Keeping a practice because it feels rigorous.
