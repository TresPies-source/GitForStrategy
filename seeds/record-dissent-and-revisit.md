# Record Dissent and Revisit Triggers

*A decision that records its dissent and its revisit triggers survives challenge.*

## What it is

Write the strongest disagreement, the decider's confidence, and the concrete events that would reopen the question into the decision record itself.

## Why it matters

Decisions are challenged later by people who were not there. A record that already holds the dissent, the confidence, and the triggers can be revisited on its merits instead of argued again from memory.

## The pattern

1. Record honest dissent, including the side that lost.
2. State the confidence plainly.
3. List revisit triggers: dates, events, observations.
4. When a trigger fires, write a new decision that supersedes the old one.

## Revisit when

A decision is expected to outlive the session that made it.

## What it refuses

Decision records written as if nobody disagreed.

## Grew from

Decision records in the Dojo Genesis practice, 2026, which carry honest dissent and explicit revisit conditions.
